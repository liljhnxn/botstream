BotStream Protocol

The Non-Custodial Recurring Subscription Protocol on EVM

1. Executive Summary

Centralized subscription gateways (Stripe, PayPal) extract 3% to 10% in processing fees, impose strict regional censorship, and reserve unilateral authority to freeze merchant earnings. In Web3, subscriptions have failed due to the native currency constraint: native gas tokens (such as ETH or BOT) cannot be debited from user wallets without a cryptographic signature. Existing solutions either force users into high-risk custodial pooled escrows or demand unlimited token allowances prone to wallet-drain exploits.

BotStream introduces an honest, non-custodial recurring subscription protocol. It allows creators and decentralized SaaS platforms to set custom subscription tiers denominated in native BOT tokens. Subscribers retain full custody of their assets, pay on a deterministic schedule, and perform one-click renewals without counterparty risk.

2. Problem Statement

2.1 Centralized Payment Gateway Monopolies

  • High Take Rates: 2.9% + $0.30 standard base fees plus foreign exchange surcharges.
  • Censorship Vulnerability: Web3 tools, DAOs, and anonymous creators are routinely unbanked or de-platformed.
  • Privacy Intrusion: Mandatory credit card and identity collection for digital memberships.

2.2 The Web3 Recurring Dilemma

  • Approval Exploit Hazards: Unlimited ERC-20 allowances create constant security vulnerabilities.
  • Custodial Lockups: Forcing users to deposit multi-month funds into protocols creates smart-contract systemic risk.

3. The BotStream Architecture

  • Native BOT Settlement: Payments route directly in native network currency without token approvals or wrapping.
  • Deterministic Lifecycle Clock: Tracks startedAt, interval, and nextPaymentTime on-chain.
  • Fair Grace Period: Overdue renewals start their fresh cycle from block.timestamp + interval, preventing penalties for missed periods.
  • Reentrancy-Guarded Creator Escrow: Creator earnings collect in isolated accounting balances, withdrawable anytime with zero middleman holding.

4. Subscription State Machine

State Condition Permissions & Access
ACTIVE block.timestamp < nextPaymentTime Full subscriber access granted.
PAYMENT_DUE block.timestamp >= nextPaymentTime Cycle lapsed; one-click renewal enabled.
CANCELED Explicit user cancellation No future billing; active until cycle concludes.
