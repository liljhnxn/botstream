## Slide 2: The Problem
### **Web3 Monetization is Broken**
* **Web2 Payment Gateways are Gatekept**: Stripe/PayPal charge 3–10%, censor Web3 companies, and freeze merchant balances without notice.
* **One-Off Crypto Donations Don't Scale**: Creators, DAOs, and SaaS tools cannot build predictable recurring revenue on volatile one-time tips.
* **The Smart Contract Approval Risk**: Existing crypto subscription attempts require unlimited ERC-20 allowances, exposing user wallets to severe drain vulnerabilities.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
### **BotStream: Transparent On-Chain Subscriptions**
* **Native Currency Payouts**: Directly priced and settled in native BOT tokens.
* **100% Non-Custodial**: Users only pay for the active period; no large lockups or fund trapping.
* **Deterministic Billing**: Clear on-chain state machine (`ACTIVE`, `PAYMENT_DUE`, `CANCELED`).
* **1-Click Renewal**: Instant wallet confirmation with fair grace-period extensions.

---

## Slide 4: How It Works
1. **Creator Creates Plan**: Sets billing cycle (e.g. 30 days), price (e.g. 10 BOT), and metadata.
2. **Subscriber Enrolls**: Pays the first cycle's fee directly via smart contract.
3. **Smart Contract Accounting**: Earnings credit instantly to creator's balance without middleman delay.
4. **Renewal Notice**: Once the cycle expires, subscriber receives a 1-click renewal alert on-chain.
